Sensor calibration

Calibration is saved on this Pi. No Mega upload required.

While calibrating, pH and clarity cannot start water replacement. Water-level protection stays active. Return both probes to the aquarium before finishing.

Readings appear when the sensors are enabled and sampling.
pH · Two-point calibration

Use stable pH ADC readings from each buffer. These readings are already averaged; do not enter the pH number.

Turbidity calibration

Enter stable ADC readings from at least two samples with known NTU values (0–1000). Higher NTU must have lower ADC. Readings use the Mega’s 100-sample average.

NTU is an interpolated estimate between references. Outside that span, no NTU number is shown; NTU mode uses the extreme clarity category on that side for protection.

Clarity ranges

Very Cloudy: critical correction. Cloudy / Slightly Cloudy: warning; an existing correction continues. Clear / Very Clear: correction stops.
